About QuimCalc

QuimCalc grew out of a simple need: having the calculation tools used every day in the lab at hand, fast and without fuss.

Who is it for?

For laboratory analysts, technicians and students working in analytical chemistry, quality control, water and wastewater treatment and related fields.

Some calculations come up again and again in daily lab work: how much of this reagent do I need to weigh? What volume do I take for the dilution? Is this result an outlier? QuimCalc answers those questions in seconds, without opening a spreadsheet or digging through old notes for the formula.

Project principles

  • ⚡
    Genuinely fast

    The site loads quickly because it uses no frameworks or external libraries: plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

  • 💸
    Free to use

    Every calculator is free and needs no sign-up. Open the page, enter your data and get the result instantly.

  • 🔒
    Your data stays on your device

    All calculations run in your browser. The values you enter are never sent to a server. See the privacy policy.

  • 📱
    Works on any device

    The layout adapts to phones, tablets and desktops, so it is just as useful on the lab computer as on your phone on the plant floor. It also works offline once installed.

  • 📖
    Explains the why

    Each tool shows the formula it uses, a worked example and frequently asked questions. It is not just a calculator: it also explains the reasoning.

Available in English

The English version focuses on method validation and quality control: calibration curves, detection and quantitation limits, measurement uncertainty, recovery and bias, outlier tests and dilution of concentrated acids. You can find them all on the home page.

More tools are being translated. The full suite, with 27 calculators and reference tables (molarity, normality, titration, Karl Fischer, buffers, periodic table, pKa table and more), is available in Spanish.

If you use QuimCalc and need a tool that is not available yet, you can suggest it on the contact page.
