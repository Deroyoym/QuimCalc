Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

QuimCalc takes privacy seriously. This page explains in plain terms what information is handled when you use the site, and how this may change as the project adds new features.

In the current version of the site

  • We do not ask for or store personally identifiable information (name, email, etc.)
  • We do not record the values you enter in the calculators
  • We do not share data with third parties for marketing purposes
  • The calculation tools do not require registration
  • We use Google Analytics to measure usage anonymously, and only if you accept cookies (see below)

How does the site work technically?

QuimCalc is a static website. All calculations run directly in your browser, without sending any data to a server. The values you enter in a calculator never leave your device.

The site has no database or backend of its own: the values you enter are not stored on any server. The only usage measurement we perform is through Google Analytics, described below.

Hosting and third-party services

The site is hosted on Vercel, a static hosting service. As with any web server, Vercel may log standard technical data such as visitors' IP addresses, the browser used and the pages visited. This information is handled exclusively by Vercel under its own privacy policy, and QuimCalc has no access to those logs.

The site loads the Source Serif 4 and DM Sans typefaces from Google Fonts, which may cause your browser to contact Google's servers. To avoid this, you can configure your browser to block requests to fonts.googleapis.com; the calculators will keep working.

Cookies and analytics

QuimCalc uses Google Analytics 4 to understand anonymously how the site is used (which pages are visited, from what type of device, etc.). For this purpose Google Analytics sets cookies in your browser, mainly the _ga cookie (and variants such as _ga_<ID>), which generates an anonymous identifier and usually lasts up to 2 years. This data is processed by Google as the service provider.

These cookies are not enabled by default. The first time you visit the site we show a notice where you can Accept or Reject analytics cookies. Google Analytics only writes the _ga cookie if you accept; we use Google's Consent Mode v2, which starts with consent denied until you decide. Your choice is stored locally in your browser so we do not ask again.

You can change your mind at any time: clear the site data in your browser to make the notice appear again, or install the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on. QuimCalc does not show ads and does not use cookies for marketing. If advertising (for example, Google AdSense) or features with user accounts are ever added, this policy will be updated beforehand.

Children

QuimCalc is a technical and educational tool. We do not collect data from anyone, including minors. The site has no age restrictions.

Changes to this policy

If a service involving personal data is ever added (for example, a contact form), this policy will be updated in advance and announced on the site. The "last updated" date at the top of this page always reflects the current version.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy or about how your information is handled, you can reach us through the contact details on the About page (in Spanish).
